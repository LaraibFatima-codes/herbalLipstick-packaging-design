# 🌿 Verdant Flora — Herbal Velvet Lipstick Packaging Design

A complete, eco-luxury product packaging and brand identity system created for a graphic design and product design internship task. This project includes an outer folding box dieline layout, inner container renders, color palette specifications, and regulatory compliance labeling.

---

## 💄 Product Overview & Brand Identity

* **Brand Name:** Verdant Flora
* **Product:** Herbal Velvet Lipstick
* **Shade:** 04 Terracotta Rose
* **Net Weight:** 4.2g / 0.15 oz.
* **Target Audience:** Modern consumers looking for clean, non-toxic, eco-conscious beauty products.

---

## 📐 Packaging Architecture & Materials

* **Outer Box:** Reverse Tuck End (RTE) 3D Folding Carton (25mm × 25mm × 85mm)
* **Stock & Finish:** 350 GSM FSC-Certified 100% Recycled Kraft Board with Soft-Touch Matte Laminate & Gold Foil Stamping
* **Inner Container:** Metallic Emerald Green Aluminium Casing with Satin Gold Collar and Shade Swatch Indicator
* **Sustainability:** 100% Vegan, Cruelty-Free, Plastic-Neutral Packaging

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Visual System

* **Deep Botanical Emerald (`#1E3A2B`):** Represents organic purity, botanical heritage, and eco-luxury.
* **Warm Sand Gold (`#D4A373`):** Premium metallic gold accents for brand logo, foil borders, and seal badges.
* **Terracotta Rose (`#C86D7D`):** Rich botanical shade representation indicator.
* **Soft Cream (`#FAF6F0`):** Soft background contrast for clear legibility on ingredient and direction panels.

---

## 📦 Packaging Panel Layout Breakdown

* **Front Panel (Hero):** Brand Logo, Product Name, Shade Name/Number, Net Weight, and "100% Natural & Vegan" guarantee.
* **Left Panel (Brand Story):** Brand ethos (*"Pure Earth, Pure Velvet"*) and clean beauty claims (0% Parabens, Synthetic Dyes, Lead).
* **Right Panel (Key Actives):** Featured organic ingredients (*Organic Neem Oil, Cold-Pressed Rosehip, Vitamin E, Shea Butter*).
* **Back Panel (Compliance):** Usage instructions, full INCI ingredient list, EAN-13 Barcode graphic, batch/expiry placeholders, and recycling symbols.
